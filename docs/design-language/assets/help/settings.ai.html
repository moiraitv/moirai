AI settings

Open Settings → AI to connect a provider. Edits stay in the tab until you choose Save in its footer. Reset asks for a second click before restoring the saved preferences. Switching Settings tabs keeps your draft.

AI provider

On the AI tab, in AI provider, choose a provider and enter its API key. The recommended model is used by default, but Advanced lets you set a different model, enable compatible web research, and choose its time limit. Choose Custom to enter your own endpoint and format. Saving checks the connection once. Disable AI retains saved credentials, and Enable AI can reconnect the selected saved provider without editing its key. See AI content selection for details.

Forget key permanently removes the selected provider’s saved credential after confirmation. Enter a replacement key to use that provider again. Disabling AI and forgetting a key are immediate actions; they do not require saving the tab.

If a connection check fails, the error stays visible so you can correct the key, endpoint, or model and retry. Existing saved credentials are not displayed in full. Backup archives include AI preferences but exclude API keys, so enter credentials again after restoring.
