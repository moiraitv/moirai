Welcome to Moirai

Moirai turns a collection of video files into on-demand, continuously available IPTV channels. You choose the media, describe how it should be arranged, and connect an IPTV application to the playlist and guide that Moirai publishes.

Moirai is a scheduling platform built on top of the ErsatzTV Next playback engine. Moirai manages your libraries and decides what plays when; ErsatzTV Next handles converting the media for playback (transcoding) and streaming it to viewers.

Moirai builds a rolling schedule from reusable pieces:

  • A Library points to media that Moirai can scan.
  • A Program chooses eligible media and decides its order.
  • A Template places Programs into parts of a day.
  • A Channel Schedule assigns Templates to a Channel.
  • A Channel provides the number, name, streaming profile, and optional logo viewers see.

The quickest route through this guide is Install with Docker, then Create your first channel.
